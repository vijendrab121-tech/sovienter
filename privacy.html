Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Sovienter Scan tumhari privacy ko sabse pehle rakhta hai. Ye page batata hai ki hum kya collect karte hain aur kya nahi karte.

1. Tumhari photo kaha jati hai?

Jab tum photo scan karte ho, PDF ya JPG banate ho, ya text nikalo (OCR) use karte ho, to tumhari image sirf tumhare phone / computer ke browser me hi process hoti hai. Hum tumhari photo ko apne server pe upload nahi karte, save nahi karte, aur kisi ke saath share nahi karte.

2. Hum kya collect karte hain?

  • Scan ki hui photo ya document - nahi collect karte
  • Tumhara naam, email, phone number - nahi mangte
  • Login / account - koi account banana nahi padta

3. Cookies aur Ads

Site ko free chalane ke liye hum Google AdSense jaise ads use kar sakte hain. Ads ke liye Google apni cookies use kar sakta hai taaki tumhe sahi ads dikhe. Tum browser ki setting me cookies band kar sakte ho.

4. Third-party services

  • PDF banane ke liye jsPDF library
  • Text nikalo (OCR) ke liye Tesseract.js
  • Hosting ke liye GitHub Pages

Ye services tumhari photo ko humare paas nahi bhejte, processing tumhare device me hi hoti hai.

5. Bachche

Ye site sabke liye hai, lekin hum jaan-bujh ke bachcho ki koi personal jaankari collect nahi karte.

6. Badlav

Agar hum is policy me badlav karenge to yahi page update kar denge. Tum kabhi bhi yaha aake latest version dekh sakte ho.

7. Contact

Privacy se related koi sawal ho to mail karo: support@sovienter.com
